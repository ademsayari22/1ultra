# Job kickoff

Record actual buyer, scope acceptance and permitted input access. Create an isolated job folder
under delivery/jobs/<id>/ only when the job exists.

1. Inspect a representative sample and list layouts, page/row count and readable fields.
2. Agree output columns, formats, identifiers, monetary rounding and source references.
3. Define duplicate/conflict rules and client treatment of ambiguous or unreadable values.
4. Estimate preparation, processing, manual checking, revision and handover time.
5. Freeze quantity, deliverables, acceptance examples, price, payment and delivery conditions.
6. Confirm that the agreed start condition is fulfilled. Record acceptance and, when required, actual deposit receipt or platform funding evidence. A payment screenshot alone is not cleared receipt.
7. Preserve originals; work on copies. Record the input version/hash and agreed scope date.
8. Assign a delivery artifact and independent review, with Adem as release owner.

Missing agreement or unreviewed input means a draft proposal, not a delivery promise.
