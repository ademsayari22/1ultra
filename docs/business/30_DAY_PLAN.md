# 30-day Tunisia lead campaign

Current focus: find Tunisian B2B leads → convince them to discuss one small paid pilot. Goals below are planning targets, not achieved buyer outcomes. The previous finance-led plan is archived in `archive/30_DAY_PLAN_2026-10-04.md`.

## Phase 0 · Find and shortlist · Days 1–3

Review five sourced companies, their Tunisian business location and published contact routes. Shortlist five companies with a plausible small document/data need. Explore accounting firms, ERP integrators, industrial distributors and digital agencies. Use the Apollo desk to set **Account HQ = Tunisia**, a relevant niche and decision-maker titles; export a bounded CSV and preview it before import. Imported provider data needs review; a published contact does not establish buying interest.

Quests: R01 source review, R02 first five selections, optional R04 another local batch. Scout Outpost gate: five reviewed sources and five relevant shortlisted companies. Preparation and import do not automatically complete quests.

## Phase 1 · Give them a reason to reply · Days 2–7

Choose one small offer that Adem can explain and check: spreadsheet cleanup, document-to-table extraction, catalogue/ERP data preparation, or a single-page website pilot after examining the problem. Capacity currently recorded: 30 hours this week; plan each day separately. Prepare a synthetic or permitted proof example and compare it with its source.

Prepare three company-specific French pitches: a verified business observation, a small proposed test and one question about their actual need or the right contact. Ask for a permitted sample before agreeing scope, price or timing. Review every claim. Apollo supplies B2B sourcing; Swokei is an external alternative for personalization and follow-ups. General Outreach fits owner-controlled templates; Smart Site Outreach is relevant to website offers. No account, mailbox, paid API or scheduler is connected in the game.

Quests: S01 offer/capacity, S02 proof, Q00 proof check, S03 three drafts, Q01 three reviews. Pitch Workshop gate: small offer, checked example and reviewed pitches. Do not transfer the court OCR accuracy measurement to unseen buyer documents.

## Phase 2 · Contact, qualify and agree one pilot · Days 3–14

Adem sends or separately authorizes a concrete reviewed message to a named recipient. Record the actual contact date, evidence and follow-up date. Record real replies, no reply, declined or qualified outcomes distinctly. Ask about current workflow, frequency, difficult fields and the person who can approve a pilot. Contacting a prospect does not establish a conversation or a sale.

Before starting, agree input permission, sample, fields, output format, exceptions, quantity, currency/price, payment terms, deadline, acceptance checks and start condition. Record actual evidence that the start condition is fulfilled.

Quests: C01 real contacts/replies, C02 bounded agreement, F04 start evidence. Conversation Hall gate: actual contact and buyer evidence, accepted pilot scope and fulfilled start condition.

## Phase 3 · Deliver a checked pilot · After agreement

Preserve inputs, link output to sources and flag ambiguous values. Check the agreed critical identifiers and amounts against their sources. Adem reviews before release. Record actual buyer acceptance, money received and whole-job effort/costs. A deposit may carry a delivery/refund obligation; currencies stay separate.

Quests: D01 output, Q02 checks, D02 release/acceptance, F05 payment/effort. Delivery Forge gate: checks, buyer acceptance, actual payment evidence and recorded effort.

## Phase 4 · Repeat or refine in Tunisia · After delivery

Ask a paying client about recurring volume and propose a capped, feasible repeat service. Use real buyer responses to refine the Tunisian niche and pitch. Expand the company list in bounded batches; keep searches inside Tunisia until Adem changes that constraint. Do not advance because of task counts, scripts or fictional rehearsals.

Quests: P01 recurring need, P02 capped proposal, R03 local evidence review. Repeat Chamber gate: confirmed repeat need and feasible follow-on scope.
