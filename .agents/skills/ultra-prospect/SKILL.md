Read data/exclusions.json and research/SNAPSHOT_2026-10-04.md before researching.
Run `python3 scripts/ultra.py script lead-audit` first. Exclusions, duplicate sources, expiry
and freshness are script work. Use an agent only for residual discovery or source interpretation,
with its limitation and judgment reason recorded. The audit does not recheck sources online.
Check whether prior leads are still relevant; do not count an old lead as newly discovered.
For new prospects, target a concrete document/data service need; source existence and contact
routes from official sites where possible. A plausible need remains a hypothesis until the
buyer confirms it. Capture company, location, URL, checked date, observed fact, contact route,
need status and next question. Return at most five strong prospects, even if research finds fewer.
Anonymous platform clients require company/exclusion and payment-history checks before bidding.
Save a dated research artifact; the coordinator adds reviewed real records. No messages/applications.
